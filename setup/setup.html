A video is missing, or only low quality is offered
  • On YouTube, click your profile picture and make sure Restricted Mode is Off.
  • If the status above says ffmpeg is missing, run the install command again. Without ffmpeg, YouTube's HD video and its audio can't be joined, so you only get 720p or lower.
  • Live streams can only be downloaded after they end. Purchased or rented movies are DRM-protected and can't be downloaded at all.
What does the install command do?

It installs yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Node.js if you don't have them, downloads ComboBreaker's helper script from its GitHub repo into your user folder, and tells your browser where to find it. Nothing runs in the background, and downloads never leave your machine.
