YouTube and some other sites need a small helper on your computer. One command installs everything.

  1. Copy this command

  2. Paste it into Terminal and press Enter

  3. Come back here

    This page notices by itself when it's done.

What does the command do?

It installs yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Node.js if you don't have them, downloads ComboBreaker's helper script from its GitHub repo into your user folder, and tells your browser where to find it. No admin rights, nothing runs in the background, and downloads never leave your machine.
