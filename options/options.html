Helper (for YouTube)

Checking…

YouTube, DASH and split-audio HLS downloads run through the free ComboBreaker Helper: a small program that runs yt-dlp and ffmpeg on this computer. Nothing leaves your machine. Twitter/X and plain video files never need it.

Passes YouTube's "sign in to confirm you're not a bot" check by downloading as your own browser session. Also unlocks age-gated and members-only videos. Off by default: YouTube has occasionally warned accounts that download heavily.

    Modify response headers as the browser receives them. Useful for loosening Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, or stripping Set-Cookie on third-party iframes.
